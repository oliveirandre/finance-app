Privacy policy

Last updated: 9 September 2026

Finance App is a private, non-commercial application operated for the personal use of its owner.

Data accessed

With explicit consent, the app accesses account information, balances, and transaction data through Enable Banking. The app does not initiate payments.

How data is used

The data is used only to display and classify personal expenses. It is not sold, shared, or made available to third parties.

Storage and security

Data is stored only in the application's private local environment. Access credentials and private keys are not exposed in the public site.

Revoking access

Consent can be withdrawn through the connected bank or Enable Banking.
